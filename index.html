Our Story

About Words
International

Words International was created with a simple yet powerful purpose — to ensure that no idea remains unheard due to lack of communication or confidence.

Founded by Hari Prasad Bokka, an R&D professional and aspiring speaker, this platform combines engineering precision and personal growth principles to help individuals improve communication and mindset.

Through structured content, podcasts, and practical insights, Words International helps people transform their thoughts into meaningful action.

"
No idea should remain unheard. Every voice, when given the right tools, has the power to change the world around it.
Hari Prasad Bokka
Founder · R&D Professional · Aspiring Speaker
